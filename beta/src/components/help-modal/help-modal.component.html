Dashboard & Organization

A
Importance-Based Columns

Tasks are grouped by Super Important, Important, and Less Important. Drag and drop tasks between these columns to instantly re-prioritize.

B
View Options

Switch between Board View (Kanban style) for high-level planning and List View for a dense, linear checklist. Use the search bar to filter visible tasks in real-time.

Execution Tools

Focus Timer

Open a task detail and click the Play button to track time automatically. The total time logged updates your analytics.

Smart Checklists

Add subtasks, drag to reorder, and now add specific notes to each subtask line. Timestamps are recorded upon completion.

When you finish a task, you'll be prompted to rate your Focus Score (1-5) and write a brief reflection. This data feeds into your Accuracy & Focus charts.

Analytics Suite

Navigate to the Analytics tab to see visual insights. Preferences for chart visibility are saved automatically.

  • Velocity: Compare tasks created vs. completed over Daily, Weekly, or Monthly ranges.
  • Accuracy: Compare your Estimated Effort vs. Actual Time logged.
  • Distribution: See where your time goes by Project and Energy Level.

Data Management

Backup & Restore

Your data is stored locally in your browser. Use the Export button in the sidebar to save a JSON backup. Use Import to restore data or move it to another device.
