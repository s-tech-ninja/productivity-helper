Smart Organization

Tasks are grouped by Super Important, Important, and Less Important. Drag and drop to re-prioritize.

Use the Smart Sort dropdown in List View to automatically order tasks by Deadline, Priority, Energy Level, or our intelligent "Smart" algorithm that highlights overdue and urgent items.

B
Performance Stats

Keep an eye on the top dashboard metrics: Daily Streak, Completion Rate, and Energy Breakdown. These indicators help you maintain momentum and balance your workload.

Execution & Recurring Tasks

Focus Timer

Track time automatically. Sessions are saved even if you close the browser. Total time logged feeds directly into your analytics.

Smart Checklists

Break tasks down. You can now add specific notes to each subtask. These notes are preserved in history for recurring tasks.

Recurring Tasks Logic

When you complete a recurring task (Daily/Weekly), it automatically resets for the next cycle. The completed instance—including its subtasks, notes, and reflection—is saved in the History tab within the task detail.

Analysis & History

Navigate to the Analytics tab for visual insights, or check the History view for a chronological log of all archived activities.

  • Completion Protocol: Upon finishing a task, rate your Focus Score (1-5) and write a reflection.
  • Velocity: Compare tasks created vs. completed over time.
  • Accuracy: Compare Estimated Effort vs. Actual Time.
  • Completed View: See all finished tasks, including today's completed recurring instances.

Data & Customization

Privacy First

Your data is stored locally in your browser. Use Export/Import in the sidebar to backup your data or move it to another device.
